ASSIGNMENT 2 STARTER PROJECTS

1) tiffintrack/ is the static website for Q1 and Q3. It includes index.html, style.css, script.js, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
2) budgetbuddy/index.html is a standalone student budget tracker for Q4.

Before deploying:
- Replace YOUR-VERCEL-DOMAIN in sitemap.xml and robots.txt after you get the real Vercel URL.
- In TiffinTrack's script.js, replace hello@example.com with an email address you control, or change the form to another real contact method.
- Botpress: create your own bot, then paste the embed snippet into index.html where the comment says to add it. Do not use a made-up snippet.
- Google Search Console: verify the live site using the available method and submit /sitemap.xml. Google may take time to index; do not claim indexing until it shows in the tool.
- Use your own real screenshots. Do not submit the sample PDF screenshots as if they were yours.

Static HTML means no framework/build command is required on Vercel.
